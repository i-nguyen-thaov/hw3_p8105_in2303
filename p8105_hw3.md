``` r

```

The NOAA dataset has 7 and 2595176 rows. The first column id gives the
station ID’s in NY, in which there are a total of 747 unique stations.
date gives us the date of a specific observation of `snow` (snowfall in
mm), `snwd` (snow depth in mm), `tmax` (the maximum temperature that day
in tenths of a degree celsius), and `tmin` (the minimum temperature that
day n tenths of a degree Celsius). There is a large amount of missing
data in tmax and tmin, where 43.7% of tmax and 43.7% of tmin are na
values.

**Data Cleaning**

``` r
clean_noaa = 
  ny_noaa |>
  janitor::clean_names() |>
  separate(date, into = c("year","month","day"), sep = "-") |>
  mutate(
    prcp = prcp / 10,
    tmax = as.numeric(tmax) / 10,
    tmin = as.numeric(tmin) / 10
  )
```

To clean, I ensured clean names, separated date into year, month, and
day using the `separate` function, and made sure that precipitation is
in mm instead of tenths of a mm to match snow and swd. tmax and tmin
were converted to Celsius instead of tenths of a Celsius as well. The
most common value for snowfall is 0, since snowfall only occurs in the
winter and this data set contains observations in all months of a year.
